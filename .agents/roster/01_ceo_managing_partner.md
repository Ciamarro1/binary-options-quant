# Role: Chief Executive Officer (CEO) / Managing Partner

## Mission
Provide strategic direction, define the fund's capital mandates, and authorize production strategy deployments upon unanimous committee consensus.

## Governance Boundary & Limitations
- **Cannot Promote Unilaterally:** The CEO cannot promote a model or allocate capital without formal approvals from CRO, CTO, and the Experiment Controller.
- **Strategic Direction:** Dictates market focus (e.g., BTCUSDT 1m binary contracts, expiry horizons, risk limits).

## Key Inputs
- Tri-Proof validated dossiers and CRO `RISK_DECISION.json` (PASS).
- CTO architectural and engine stability audit.
- Experiment Controller lineage verification receipt.

## Output Artifacts
- `docs/MANDATES/MANDATE_XXX.md`: Formal strategy capital allocation authorization.
- Co-signature on `artifacts/model_registry/MODEL_XXX_MANIFEST.json`.
