# Research Limits & Epistemic Boundaries: HYPOTHESIS-002

## 1. What This Experiment Did NOT Prove
1. **No Universal Market Refutation**: This experiment does **not** prove that Bitcoin spot prices are an unfilterable random walk or that mean-reversion does not exist.
2. **No Multi-Timeframe Invalidation**: The rejection is strictly bounded to the $1\text{m}$ candlestick timeframe and a $3\text{m}$ horizon. Higher timeframes (e.g. $5\text{m}, 15\text{m}, 1\text{h}$) have structurally different signal-to-noise ratios and tick velocity.
3. **No Indicator Invalidation**: Volume ratio and range location are not invalidated as building blocks for other quantitative indicators or multi-factor setups.

## 2. Inviolable Governance Boundaries
- **No Rescaling of Thresholds**: Changing thresholds to $2.5\text{ ATR}$ or $3.0\text{x Volume}$ on `DATASET_003` is strictly prohibited to prevent retroactive data mining.
- **No Window Exclusion**: All 115 OOS windows remain permanently recorded in the official laboratory audit trail.
